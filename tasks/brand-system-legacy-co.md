## 2. Molecules (Identity)

### Logos
- **Primary wordmark** — "Legacy Co" set in Archivo Black
- **Monogram** — "LC" stacked or paired
- **Full lockup with tagline** — "Legacy Co — The Brand Bridge Framework"
- **Vertical / stacked variants**
- **Co-brand lockup** — TBD per partnership; default is "Legacy Co × [Partner]" with vertical bar separator
- **Sizing rule** — min 80px width digital / 0.75in print; clear space = cap height of "L" on all sides
- **Substitution targets** — Open Session Figma kit's `Logo / Primary`, `Logo / Mark`, `Logo / Lockup` containers

### Type system applied
See atoms scale. Display tier is monolithic Archivo Black; body tier is Helvetica Neue 400/500/700. No italic Archivo Black ever (Archivo Black has no real italic — synthetic italic looks broken).

### Color applied
- **Primary surface:** mono-950 (#080808) with brand-500 (#C9A84C) accent
- **Alt surface:** brand-50 cream (#F5E6C8) with mono-950 type
- **Hero emphasis:** gold gradient on display type
- **Banned:** brand-500 type on mono-25 — fails contrast (use brand-700+ or mono-950 on light surfaces)

### Art direction — four creative territories

| Territory | Mood | Palette constraint | Photographic / visual rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heritage** | Warm, archival, weighty | Brand 50→500 + mono 700→950 | Grain, sepia tinting permissible; vintage objects, archival textures; *not* nostalgic — provenance, not yearning |
| **Quiet Authority** | Minimal, high contrast | Mono 950 + brand 500 + mono 25 | Single subject, environmental portraits of founders/leaders in their context; no group shots; soft natural light |
| **Crafted Ritual** | Tactile, close, deliberate | Brand 50→300 + mono 600→950 | Hands-at-work, tools, process shots; macro distance; warm light |
| **Modern Provenance** | Architectural, present | Mono 950 + brand 700 | Buildings, materials, weighty surfaces; long lens; not futuristic, not retro — *now* |

### Illustrations
- **Style** — line work + flat fills; architectural / monumentation motifs (columns, archways, foundations) reading "legacy as durable infrastructure"
- **Line behavior** — 1.5–2.5px even-weight stroke; no taper; rounded caps
- **Palette constraint** — max 3 colors per illustration; always include one mono

### Textures
- **Brass leaf / hammered metal** — used at ≤20% opacity; never as primary surface
- **Aged paper grain** — editorial and long-form contexts only
- **Stone / concrete** — architectural / Modern Provenance territory only
- **Restraint rule** — one texture per composition. Never two.

## 3. Organisms

### Brand Guidelines

#### 01 — Brand Messaging
- **Mission:** Help founders and leaders build authentic, lasting brands.
- **Voice:** confident, grounded, deliberate. Name what is true. Do not chase trends.
- **Tone modifiers:** warmer in onboarding, sharper in pricing and strategy, quietest in delivery moments.
- **Banned words:** *cutting-edge, disrupt, synergy, next-gen, game-changing, revolutionize, leverage* (the verb), *unlock* (overused).
- **Signature phrases:** "Brand Bridge," "rooted in identity, clarity, and purpose," "build something that lasts."
- **How we talk about AI:** AI is a tool, not the work. We use AI to amplify craft, not replace it. We don't pretend AI work is hand-craft, and we don't pretend craft is AI work.
- **Messaging hierarchy:** Identity → Clarity → Purpose → Activation. Every page, deck, or pitch should resolve to one of these four.

#### 02 — Brand Identity
- **Logo rules:** min 80px width; never on busy backgrounds without solid panel; gold mark on dark surfaces, mono-950 mark on cream/light surfaces.
- **Color application:** primary surface = mono-950 + brand-500 accent. Never brand-500 type on mono-25 (fails contrast).
- **Typography hierarchy:** see molecules. Archivo Black only on display tier; never as body.
- **Do:** dark + gold + cream; gold gradient for CTA / hero emphasis; restrained gold rule lines as dividers.
- **Don't:** rainbow gradients; italicized Archivo Black; gold-on-light without darker support; mixing more than two display weights in one composition.

#### 03 — Art Direction
See molecules. Each of the four territories gets a do/don't board. Photography prefers real founders and real locations over stock — when stock is unavoidable, use the territory's palette constraint as a filter. Illustration is functional, not decorative — only when it amplifies the message.

#### 04 — AI Usage
- **LLM context.** This guidelines document is itself the MCP context. Keep it parsable: structured headings, JSON token blocks, no decorative formatting that obscures the rules.
- **Image generation prompt pattern:** `[territory name] photography of [subject], [palette constraint], [restraint cue]` — e.g. *"Quiet Authority photography of a founder at her desk, mono-950 environment with brand-500 accent, single subject, soft natural light, 35mm grain."*
- **Voice for AI-generated copy:** same as Brand Messaging. Run every output through the banned-words list. AI drafts always reviewed by a human before shipping.
- **Code agents:** design tokens live as CSS variables in `:root` (`--gold`, `--cream`, `--black`, etc.). Agents reading the codebase should map to those variable names, never to raw hex literals — variables carry semantic intent that hex codes lose.
- **Honesty rule:** if AI assisted, the human reviewer is named in the file's commit. We don't hide AI use, and we don't fake hand-craft.

#### 05 — Brand Activation
Concrete blueprints — not mood boards.

- **Web** — gold-on-black home; gradient on hero display type; dense grid of partner/client logos in `mono-50` opacity-10; Archivo Black display, Helvetica Neue body.
- **Social (carousel)** — 10 slides max; brand-500 accent on mono-950 surface; white type or gold-gradient for hero slide; signature mark bottom-right slide 1 + slide 10 only.
- **Print: business card** — mono-950 face, brand-500 mark, mono-25 type, mono-50 reverse for contact info.
- **Print: letterhead** — brand-50 (cream) ground, mono-950 type, single brand-500 rule line at 24px below masthead.
- **Motion** — `--grad-gold-h` shimmer (existing keyframe `shimmer`) on CTAs only; fades over slides; never bouncy or playful easing.
- **Email** — mono-950 background, brand-500 rule lines, brand-50 body type, brand-500 CTA pill.
- **One-pager** — mono-950 cover with brand-500 mark; brand-50 interior with mono-950 type; monumental display headers (Archivo Black, presentation tier).
